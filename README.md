**Velvet Vault** is a top-down stealth heist game: a lone thief slips through three museum rooms — the Emerald Gallery, the Gold Vault and the Crown Chamber — lifting gems, gold bars, paintings and wall safes while guards patrol with sweeping vision cones and CCTV cameras sweep the floor. Every room has several routes behind display cases and plinths, and every tool costs points: an EMP to black out the cameras, a smoke bomb to vanish from every cone, a lockpick to pop a safe open at once. An alarm meter climbs while anyone sees you and falls when you break sight; at full alarm the house locks down and every guard converges. Lift the required loot, reach the green exit, and get away clean before the clock runs out. All art is drawn in code and all sound is synthesized with WebAudio — no files, no network, fully offline.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — or just open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser. Move with WASD or arrow keys, hold Shift to sneak, hold E to crack a safe, and press 1 / 2 / 3 for EMP, smoke and lockpick. P pauses, M mutes.
